## 1. Vision & Problem Solved
Predy is a Jarvis-like, economically accountable AI co-pilot for DeFi yield optimization. It combines real-world semantic analysis, internal prediction markets, privacy-preserving reasoning, and scoped on-chain execution while enforcing skin-in-the-game accountability via VeriBond staking/slashing.

**Core Problem**: Users lose money to opaque yield volatility with no transparent reasoning, no safety guarantees, and no economic accountability from AI agents.

**Predy Solution**: A visible, interactive, reputation-backed multi-agent swarm that predicts, debates, stakes, protects, and safely acts — with full human oversight + autonomy.
